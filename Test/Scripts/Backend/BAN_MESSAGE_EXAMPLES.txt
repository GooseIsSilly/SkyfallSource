BEST PRACTICES FOR BAN DURATIONS
================================================================================

First Offense (Minor):
----------------------
1-3 days
"First warning - Review our rules"

First Offense (Major):
----------------------
7-14 days
"Serious violation - Next ban is longer"

Second Offense:
---------------
14-30 days
"Second strike - Final warning"

Third Offense:
--------------
Permanent
"Third strike - Account permanently banned"

Immediate Permanent:
--------------------
Use for:
- Severe harassment
- Hate speech
- Confirmed cheating with hardware bans
- Real money trading
